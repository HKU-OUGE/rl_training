Figure 3 Single-Pass Evaluation Protocol N=2000 · T=500 (10 s) · cmd_vx=1.0 m/s · heading=0
Figure 3. Eval protocol. (a) 2000 envs are spawned in a 30-difficulty × 12-sub-terrain grid with curriculum locked (curriculum=False, max_init_terrain_level=29), yielding 5–6 envs per cell evenly distributed. Cells inherit their type from the terrain generator's proportional layout; color encodes difficulty (top: easy → bottom: hard). (b) Within each 8 m × 8 m tile the robot spawns at the leftmost anchor facing +x. The velocity command is fixed at 1.0 m/s with heading lock (heading=0). An episode ends when the robot's +x displacement crosses 4 m (reached_goal, success) or any of the natural termination conditions fires (time-out at 10 s, illegal base contact, or terrain bound exit). All domain randomization (friction, mass, push, etc.) is disabled so that comparisons across cells reflect policy behaviour, not environmental variance.
