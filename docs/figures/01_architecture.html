Figure 1. SplitMoE teacher policy. Three observation groups (proprioception, noisy elevation + multi-pitch LiDAR, history) are independently encoded; latent representations are concatenated (251-D) and processed by a single-layer GRU (hidden 256). LayerNorm-projected RNN state feeds two independent gating networks that emit softmax weights over 6 leg experts and 3 wheel experts. Each expert is an MLP[256, 128, 128] whose outputs are mixed by the gate to produce a 12-D joint-position command (legs) and a 4-D joint-velocity command (wheels), concatenated into the 16-D action vector. A separate critic GRU is used only at training time.
